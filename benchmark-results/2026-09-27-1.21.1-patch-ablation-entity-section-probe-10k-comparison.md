## Allocation measurement limitation

Allocation profiling could not be quantified on this Windows/JDK setup: HotSpot reported an active JFR recording, but `jcmd JFR.dump` returned “0 bytes written” for both startup and live dumps. Therefore this report makes no allocation-rate claim. Process CPU was higher with the patch (116.1% vs 113.2% of one core); RAM remained effectively flat.
